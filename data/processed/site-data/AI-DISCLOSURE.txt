AI disclosure (NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, team Hidden Planners)

The data in this folder is AI-assisted: AI-written code computed it.

- Generated by: the data pipeline in src/pipeline/ of
  https://github.com/dhidru/nasa-space-apps-2026, written with the AI coding
  assistant Claude Code (Anthropic, model Claude Opus 5.5) at the team's
  direction.
- Computed from: NASA LRO LOLA elevation models (PDS), NASA/JPL NAIF SPICE
  kernels and USGS Artemis candidate landing region outlines. See
  ATTRIBUTIONS.md in the repository for sources, licenses and citations.
- No value here was written or made up by an AI model. Every number comes from
  the pipeline's calculations on the source data.
- The base-map PNGs (basemap.png) are hillshades computed from LOLA
  elevations. They weren't drawn by an image-generation model.

Details: the "Use of AI" section of README.md and the AI usage log in
ATTRIBUTIONS.md.
